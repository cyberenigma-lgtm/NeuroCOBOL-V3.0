## 🟦 1. Control Statements & Cognitive Swarm Directives

- `PROGRAM [Name]`: Registers a process as a citizen in the **NeuroBUS**.
- `DATA / LOGIC`: Declaration and execution blocks.
- `IMPORT [Module]`: Imports defense, security, or AI modules (`IMPORT DEFENSE.INTEGRITY`).
- `AGENT [Name] HEMISPHERE [LEFT|RIGHT]`: Registers a micro-agent into Left (Logic/AST) or Right (Creative/Audio) brain queues.
- `EVALUATE HYPOTHESIS [Name]`: Triggers System 2 CoT structured hypothesis reasoning.
- `VECTOR SEARCH [Query] TOP_K [N]`: Executes sub-millisecond native k-NN vector queries.
- `ACTIVATE SIMD DVTRGAS30`: Switches hardware execution to Ring-0 C/Rust AVX2/FMA vector engine.
- `COMPRESSED (COMP-3)`: High-precision BCD financial arithmetic.
